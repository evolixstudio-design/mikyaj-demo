## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end Gulf beauty commerce: uncompromising precision, tactile stillness, and editorial prestige. Tailored explicitly for an elite Kuwaiti clientele, the aesthetic reconciles global luxury minimalism with regional prestige. The emotional response is one of quiet indulgence, gallery-grade composure, and absolute trustworthiness.

The visual direction draws from high-fashion editorial publishing and sculptural architecture. It rejects loud, hyper-saturated promotional noise in favor of generous, airy negative space, immaculate proportional balance, and subtle warmth. The interface acts as an architectural plinth—letting formulation pigments, bottle textures, and olfactory storytelling take precedence. 

Every interaction honors both English and Arabic typographic dignity, ensuring bi-directional symmetry without sacrificing typographic hierarchy or visual sophistication.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in dark, moody sophistication and high contrast clarity:

- **Primary (`#121212`)**: An obsidian charcoal rather than a synthetic pure black. Used for high-impact editorial headings, actionable CTA surfaces, and crisp framing elements.
- **Secondary (`#C98E84`)**: An understated, sun-warmed desert rose blush. Used exclusively for selective highlights, active pill fills, subtle hover affirmations, and special editorial curation tags.
- **Tertiary (`#7D6B62`)**: A warm stone taupe delivering softened metadata, 3-decimal currency symbols (`KWD`), and category attributions.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF8F5`)**: A curated alabaster/warm ivory base that softens harsh screen glare and provides a cosmetic linen canvas. Supplementary canvas tones include pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated product staging cards and micro-border borders in warm bone (`#EAE5DF`).

Color accessibility strictly exceeds WCAG AAA for editorial content, ensuring thin display letterforms maintain crisp readability against dark backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-fashion editorial grandeur with modern, geometric legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Evokes the heritage of haute perfumery and beauty journals. Headings are light-to-regular weight, maintaining extreme vertical poise and high stroke contrast.
- **Body & Functional UI (`DM Sans`)**: Provides crisp, neutral, geometric balance. Its geometry avoids sterile technicism, bringing warmth and openness to Arabic and Latin scripts alike.
- **KWD Currency Formatting**: Kuwaiti Dinar displays always format to three decimal places (e.g., `48.500 KWD`). The integer sits at `price-primary`, while the fraction and currency code (`.500 KWD`) sit scaled down to `price-fraction` in tertiary stone to avoid jarring numeric visual weight.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony**: When rendered in Arabic, titles match the stroke weight and vertical majesty of Bodoni Moda, while utility interfaces utilize aligned geometric counters for uninterrupted lateral reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, gallery-style 12-column desktop grid centered within a `1440px` maximum bounding box. Generous outer margins (`4rem`) create breathing room, anchoring the catalog as an upscale boutique catalog rather than a crowded transactional matrix.

- **Desktop Standard**: 12 columns with 32px gutters and 64px outer margins. Modular grids support 3-column product presentations (spanning 4 columns each) for editorial focus, or 4-column arrangements (3 columns each) for full seasonal collections.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous sectional separation uses `space-3xl` (72px) and `space-4xl` (96px) to demarcate curated brand edits, sensory scent notes, and shade pickers without dense horizontal dividers.
- **Bilingual Flow**: The grid structure supports seamless direction mirroring (`dir="rtl"`). Gutters, margin insets, and editorial column blocks invert across the vertical axis with strict proportional parity.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through tonal layering and razor-sharp, low-contrast micro-borders rather than heavy ambient blur or drop shadows:

- **Zero Heavy Shadows**: The design system bans standard multi-stop drop shadows. Elevation must feel flat, crisp, and paper-like.
- **Micro-Borders ("L'Arête")**: Structural edges utilize a 1px hairline border tinted with `#EAE5DF` on light ivory and `#E2DCD5` on white cards. These borders define separation without cluttering whitespace.
- **Elevated Floating Drawers & Overlays**: Cart flyouts, shade finder modals, and navigation ribbons use an ivory surface layer (`#FAF8F5`) elevated merely by an ultra-diffused, micro-ambient veil: `box-shadow: 0 12px 40px -10px rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.04)`.
- **Image Canvases**: Photography is anchored within clean `#F4F0EB` aspect-ratio containers, granting tactile silhouette relief to translucent perfume bottles and gilded compact cases.

## Shapes

The design system employs a sharp, architectural shape language (`roundedness: 0`):

- **Architectural Edge**: UI cards, dialog surfaces, button outlines, and thumbnail containers utilize strict `0px` radius (sharp corners). This geometry reflects luxury packaging boxes, monolithic marble storefronts, and printed editorial spreads.
- **Intentional Contrast via Circular Cosmetic Swatches**: The sole exception to the rectangular rule is cosmetic shade selection swatches and formulation drops, which exist as pure geometric circles to communicate tactile, liquid reality against the razor-sharp container grid.
- **Badges and Status Tags**: Always cut as razor-straight rectangles, preserving a couture runway tag feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Obsidian background (`#121212`), pure white text, 0px border-radius, minimum 48px height, padded with `1.5rem` laterally. Lettering set in uppercase `label-caps`. Hover states subtly lighten to a deep velvet charcoal (`#2A2827`) with smooth 200ms transition.
- **Secondary Action**: Transparent background with a crisp 1px hairline outline (`#121212`), charcoal text. Hover inverts the button gracefully to solid charcoal with white text.
- **Tertiary (Editorial Link)**: No border. Text link anchored by an animated 1px hairline underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Staging**: Housed on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sand (`#F5F1EB`) backgrounds, wrapped in a 1px border (`#EAE5DF`).
- **Media Presentation**: 4:5 vertical aspect ratio. On hover, the primary product shot cross-fades smoothly (400ms ease) into a secondary editorial skin swatch or lifestyle application view.
- **Floating Actions**: Quick-add and shade preview trigger via an understated floating bottom shelf within the image frame, appearing only on cursor focus.
- **Price Typography**: Kuwaiti Dinar values feature full 3-decimal precision (`18.250 KWD`), aligning the fractional decimals cleanly to ensure optical stability.

### Chips & Filter Badges
- Unselected filters use an off-white fill (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#EAE5DF`) with taupe text.
- Active filters invert cleanly into obsidian charcoal (`#121212`) with white text and an explicit micro 'close' icon.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Text inputs forgo boxed containers in favor of an architectural bottom border (1px hairline in `#D4CDC5`).
- Active and focused states deepen the underline to `#121212` with floating label transitions using `label-caps`. Inputs carry generous vertical padding (14px top/bottom).

### Bilingual Language & Currency Switcher
- Sits unobtrusively within the header utility rail. Displays current locale (`EN / KWD` or `العربية / د.ك`) with a pristine typographic toggle separated by a hair-thin vertical bar.
