Questions about shopping with Mikyaj

How much is delivery in Kuwait?

Delivery is free when the product subtotal before discounts is 10.000 KWD or more. Below 10 KWD, delivery costs 1, 2 or 3 KWD depending on your area. Select your area to see the charge before placing your order.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery normally takes 1–3 days. Your account or private tracking link shows the order status. For help with an address or an existing order, contact Mikyaj on WhatsApp with your order number.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available at Mikyaj Kuwait. Review the product total and any delivery charge at checkout before placing your order. Online payment is available only when shown at checkout.

Where is the Mikyaj Kuwait shop?

Mikyaj Kuwait is a beauty shop in Souk Al-Mubarakiyah, operated by Burhan al dahabi gen trad co. Visit Shop 10, Gharabally Souq Street, Kuwait City. Call +965 6623 2231 to check visit arrangements before travelling.
